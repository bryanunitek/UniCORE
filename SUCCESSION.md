## Scope extension to UniCORE

The UniCORE name surface and the open list of `UniCORE.<Industry>` classifications inherit the succession arrangement set out in the canonical document.

Two structural points carry through to this repository:

1. **The gift principle is not subject to succession.** The CC BY 4.0 licence has already been given; succession is about stewardship of the name, the structural rules, and the published documentation, not about the gift surface itself.
2. **Industry-classified sibling repositories** (`UniCORE.Law`, `UniCORE.Healthcare`, etc.) when created will carry forward the same succession arrangement.
